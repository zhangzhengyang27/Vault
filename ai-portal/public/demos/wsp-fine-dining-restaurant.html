Our Philosophy

We cook over live fire because it demands attention. Every cut is dry-aged in-house, every sauce built from bones roasted that morning, every plate finished by hand.

The menu is short on purpose. Fewer dishes, done completely — from farms we name on the menu to the salt in the butter.
