About

Built by hand,
run on kindness.

Groundwork opened in 2019 when Jamie Walsh swapped a decade of accountancy for a second-hand espresso machine and a lease on a rainy corner in Ancoats. The spreadsheet habit stuck — the coffee got a lot better.
