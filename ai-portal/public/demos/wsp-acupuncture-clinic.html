The science of acupuncture.

TCM sees the body as a network of meridians carrying qi. Hover a point to meet six of the most requested.

How acupuncture works.

In Traditional Chinese Medicine, health reflects the smooth movement of qi along meridians — a lattice of channels beneath the skin. When qi stalls, symptoms surface.

Modern research suggests acupuncture influences endogenous opioid release, autonomic nervous system regulation and inflammatory markers — mechanisms your body already owns.

These six points are a fraction of the 361 classical locations. Your selection is always individual — mapped to your pattern, not your diagnosis code.

Your first visit.

1

The Consultation 30 minutes

A detailed health history — symptoms, sleep, digestion, mood, energy, menstrual health where relevant. Tongue diagnosis and a three-position pulse reading.

2

Your Treatment Plan 10 minutes

Your TCM diagnosis explained in plain English: the pattern, the cause, and a realistic course. We recommend a number of sessions and say why. No pressure, ever.

3

The Treatment 45–60 minutes

Rest on a warmed treatment bed. Hair-fine, single-use needles placed gently — most people feel almost nothing, or a dull warmth as the qi arrives. Needles stay 20–30 minutes; most patients doze.

4

Ongoing Support between sessions

After each visit: diet, lifestyle, movement or breathing suggestions. A formal review every four sessions to adjust the plan.
