Getting Here

Two hours from London, five minutes from Bourton-on-the-Water — the heart of the Cotswolds, without the tourist crowds.

By car

M40 junction 10, then the A361 through Burford. Free parking behind the house — electric chargers on the wall nearest the kitchen garden.

By train

Direct from London Paddington to Kingham (80 minutes). We'll collect you from the platform if you tell us you're coming.
